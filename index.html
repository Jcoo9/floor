What we do

Three ways to build the floor.

The sales layer can stand on its own. The operating layer can stand on its own. When the opportunity crosses both, we build them as one system.

01

Fractional Sales Leadership

Embedded sales management for companies that need experienced leadership without adding a full-time sales executive.

  • Seller coaching and development
  • Pipeline and opportunity reviews
  • Accountability and management cadence
  • Sales culture and team alignment
  • Hiring, onboarding, and playbook development
02

Fractional COO / Operating Layer

Hands-on operating structure for founders and teams that need a stronger management rhythm between strategy and daily execution.

  • Roles, ownership, and decision paths
  • Weekly operating rhythm and reporting
  • Workflow and handoff design
  • Systems, tooling, and automation
  • Cross-functional operating discipline
03

Sales Operations Layer

A complete sales operations layer that connects how the company finds opportunity, prepares people, runs meetings, follows through, advances pipeline, and learns from the work.

  • Target market and offer structure
  • Prospecting and account intelligence
  • Meeting prep, follow-up, and proposals
  • Pipeline stages and next-action discipline
  • Proof capture and reusable learning
Built to stand alone or integrate. The sales operations layer can be a durable management layer on its own, or it can connect into marketing, delivery, product, systems, finance, and leadership when the company needs a broader build.
