Where revenue leaks

More leads are not always the first answer.

Before spending more money to create demand, it is worth asking whether revenue is already being lost inside the business.

Stalled deals

Opportunities stay open without a real next step, owner or deadline.

Dormant customers

Former buyers disappear from view even though the relationship still has value.

Underworked accounts

Good customers could buy more, but nobody truly owns account growth.

Founder bottlenecks

Too many important deals still need the founder to approve, rescue or close them.

Unmanaged sellers

People are selling, but nobody consistently owns pipeline, coaching and accountability.

Partnerships without ownership

Referral, channel and strategic opportunities exist, but nobody is driving them.

Revenue ideas that never launch

New packages, products, services or monetization ideas get discussed and then stall.

AI without a real operating model

Tools get added before anyone decides what people should own, what machines should help with, and how the work should flow.
