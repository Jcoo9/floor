In many small businesses, the owner is still reviewing pipeline, rescuing deals, motivating sellers, setting priorities, handling performance issues and deciding what revenue opportunity to pursue next.

The Floor helps put structure around that work — and helps transfer it to the right internal person when the business is ready.
