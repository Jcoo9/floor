The founder test

If your sellers report straight to ownership, somebody is doing the sales-manager job. Usually it is the founder.

  • Running the weekly sales meeting
  • Chasing next steps on important deals
  • Coaching and motivating reps
  • Deciding what is actually in the forecast
  • Keeping follow-up and accountability alive

Everybody needs a coach.

Experienced sellers still need feedback, motivation, communication, deal help and accountability. The role of a sales manager is not to hover over professionals. It is to help good sellers perform better and create enough separation that every sales issue does not land directly on the founder.

Who this is for.

The Floor is strongest when there is already a real sales motion—but management is too dependent on the founder, too junior or temporarily missing.

The founder is still managing sales.

The company is between sales leaders.

The permanent search may take months. The team still needs management now.

The current manager is too junior.

The team is active but inconsistent.
