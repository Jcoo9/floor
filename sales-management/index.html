What week one looks like

We do not spend three weeks “getting oriented.”

The goal is to understand the team and pipeline quickly enough to begin managing during the first week.

Day 1

Founder download

Goals, team, current pressure, problem deals, customers, forecast and where you are still getting pulled in.

Day 2–3

Team + pipeline

Meet the sellers, inspect the pipeline and understand how people are actually working today.

Day 3–4

Priorities

Identify the deals, people and process issues that deserve immediate attention.

Day 5

Cadence starts

Sales meeting, 1:1 rhythm, forecast structure and follow-up expectations are already in motion.

The operating cadence

Simple enough to run every week. Strong enough to change behavior.

A typical week

The founder should not have to invent the management system.

The exact cadence varies by team, but the work should become predictable.

Monday

Sales meeting

Top priorities, pipeline movement, important deals and commitments for the week.

During week

Rep 1:1s + deal coaching

Performance, skill development, obstacles and the opportunities that need help now.

Ongoing

Pipeline + follow-up

Make sure next steps are real, stale deals are confronted and commitments do not disappear.

Founder update

Forecast + decisions

What is likely to close, what needs intervention and where the founder actually needs to be involved.

What should change

After 30–60 days, the founder should feel less necessary to the daily sales operation.

Cleaner pipeline

Fewer fantasy deals, clearer next steps and a more useful view of where revenue is actually coming from.

Better rep accountability

People know what matters, what they committed to and when they will be held accountable for it.

Less founder rescue work

The founder gets pulled into the deals and decisions that require them — not every gap in the operating system.
