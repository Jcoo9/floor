Common questions

What founders usually want to know before starting.

Do I need an existing sales team?

The best fit is a founder-led B2B company with a handful of people already touching revenue. The Floor is strongest when there is something real to manage, prioritize and improve.

Are you replacing our VP Sales?

Not necessarily. The Floor can manage through a gap, support a founder who is not ready to hire permanently, or help stabilize the operation while a recruiter runs the search.

Will you actually sell?

The engagement is commercial and hands-on. We may reactivate customers, pursue partners, tighten deals, build offers or open new lanes. The goal is not to sit outside the operation and comment on it.
